# DrivePool Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

DrivePool is a personal Android app by Redowan Khan that shows your own
Google Drive accounts' storage in one pooled dashboard.

## What DrivePool accesses

- **Google Drive, read-only** (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly`).
  DrivePool reads only your account email, storage quota (used / total),
  file count, and the total size of files modified today. It cannot edit,
  upload, or delete any file.

## What DrivePool stores

- **On your phone only:** your account email and the quota numbers above,
  saved in the app's private storage so the dashboard loads offline.
- **Access tokens are held in memory only** and are never stored on disk.
- **No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking.** DrivePool talks
  directly to Google's servers. No data is sent to the developer or to
  any third party.

## Sign-in

Sign-in uses Google Sign-In through the DrivePool Google Cloud project.
You can revoke DrivePool's access at any time from your Google Account
settings (Third-party access), and you can remove a linked account from
inside the app by long-pressing its card.
